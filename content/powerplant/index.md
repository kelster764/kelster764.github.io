A fun mini puzzle game with interactables.
-
This powerplant puzzle game in Unreal is an experience I designed myself from beginning to end. I designed the level, designed the art, and coded the gameplay.

The main thing I want to show for this game is my abilities to create a user experience. Most of the coding handled whether or not players are hitting buttons in the correct order or not, and what objects are in the inventory. (and is a predecessor for code seen in Lantern of Souls)

My idea for this game was to create a point and click experience similar to puzzle games I loved such as Poptropica, while also being satisfying with clicks and clacks on the buttons. 

The Center Console
-
The gameplay is centered on the center console. After each puzzle is solved, another light on the console turns green. 
![alt text](<handle.png>)
![alt text](<console2.gif>)

The first puzzle you're most likely to solve is the broken handle. When you are first spawned, you are prompted to add a handle on the floor to your inventory. When you are in front of the console, you drag and drop the handle to the console and PULL THE LEVER.  

![alt text](<blackboard.png>)
![alt text](<console1.gif>)

The second puzzle you might solve is the keypad. When you go to the right of the board, there is a blackboard next to the bookshelf that has a code that Jeffrey decided to write up there. Use that to type in your number and get the next light.

![alt text](<casette.png>)
![alt text](<console3.gif>)
 
 The last puzzle is the soundboard. It is done by going to the casette player and dropping the casette in it. The casette plays an audio that you then try to match on the sound buttons on the console. 

Thoughts
